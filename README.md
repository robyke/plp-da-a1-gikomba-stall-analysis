## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and wants to understand how her business is performing. This analysis uses 14 days of sales data and current stock information to measure overall sales, compare Week 1 with Week 2, identify strong and weak sales days, compare weekday and weekend performance, check products that need restocking and calculate product gross margins.

## How to Run

Open the `stall_analysis.ipynb` notebook in Google Colab: **[Open in Colab](YOUR-COLAB-LINK-HERE)**

## Top Three Findings

1. Total sales for the 14 days were **KES 46,450**, with average daily sales of **KES 3,317.86**.
2. Sales increased from **KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2**, representing growth of approximately **10.7%**.
3. Weekend sales averaged **KES 4,900**, about **82.5% higher** than the weekday average of **KES 2,685**, while **Tomatoes and Sukuma** need to be restocked.
